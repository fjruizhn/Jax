# Service lifecycle
## Purpose
Safe lifecycle operations.
## Scope
Observed JAX services.
## Preconditions
Identify unit and current state.
## Authority impact
None; do not amend authority.
## Safe procedure
Use approved service-manager commands and inspect diagnostics.
## Verification
Query health/status after operation.
## Fail-closed condition
Unknown unit or integrity failure: stop.
## Recovery / escalation
Escalate to operator.
## Prohibited actions
Do not bypass policy or invent units.
